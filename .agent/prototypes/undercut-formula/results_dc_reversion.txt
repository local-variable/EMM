  Negative = a World trading above the reference falls back afterwards.
  'partial DC' is the DC gap's correlation with what happened next AFTER the
  World's own deviation from its 180-day level is accounted for. That is the
  column that matters: the first two overlap heavily with each other.

  Why two panels: in 8a the same 30-day median sits in the denominator of both
  predictors and the numerator of the outcome, so noise in that one estimate
  drags the correlation negative by itself. 8b builds the predictors from the
  older half of the window and measures the outcome against the newer half, so
  no estimate is shared. Where 8a and 8b disagree, 8b is the honest number and
  the difference between them is the size of the artefact.

  Caveats that neither panel fixes: one 30-day forward window on one World, so
  this is a single observation of a relationship rather than an estimate of its
  stability; and the Aether median is itself dominated by whichever worlds
  upload most.
