Bloodstained — Sell Items (20 postings)
The sell space is the Retainer's stock The Player drags a box over the Retainer's own inventory. Marked slots turn green and EMM may sell what sits in them. Unmarked slots are outside its reach entirely.
The board is the consequence, not the space The sell list beside it is what the allocation produced — EMM picks which of the granted stock takes the 20 postings, most revenue first. It is shown here because it is related, never because it is the thing being marked.
Retainers have seven pages The space is marked per page and totals across them, so a Retainer whose stock is spread does not have to be tidied first.
Three ways to mark, all of them supported Click one slot to toggle it · Shift-click to take everything between this slot and the last one · Drag a box over any run of them. Marking is per page and totals across all seven.
Slot count drives the Strategy Not just capacity. Many slots reads as a commodity and assigns accordingly; one slot reads as a single sale. The assignment follows what is entering and available, and stays overridable per Ware.
Dropping stock in is the enrolment Put something into a green slot and it is enrolled — the last manual chore set-and-forget left standing. On backing out, the aggregate rolls up into the Sell Space tab, shown on demand.
