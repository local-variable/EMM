- [Design the price history graph and its confidence bands](https://github.com/local-variable/EMM/issues/12) — **The graph is a view over the pricing engine: its trend line is the same estimate the pricer quotes, evaluated day by day, and every honesty rule the estimator carries is drawn rather than footnoted.** Worked as a visual prototype on fifteen real Wares from the cached Cactuar corpus, all six answers approved point by point. **Series:** Sales as dots sized by Stack, Listings as lines from when each was put up plus a board ladder, never the same mark; the estimate is the trend line. **"Confidence bars" resolves to two bands that cannot be confused and a third that must not exist:** *where Sales landed* (middle 50% / 80%, a soft fill) and *precision of the estimate* (an 80% block-bootstrap interval, dotted edges) — and **no predicted range**, because the undercut-formula ticket showed an interval measures estimation error and says almost nothing about the next Sale; the legend says "not a forecast" in words. **Sparse data looks thin by construction:** dots always drawn, the band absent below 5 effective observations, the estimate line breaking where no Sales rung applies and dashed where provisional, candles (optional) tick-only under three Sales, **Freshness hatched onto the time axis** as "no upload for …", the start of held History marked, and the severity ladder as a chip and warning box. **HQ vs NQ is an overlay** (NQ · HQ · Both), colours fixed to the entity, never a facet. **Time:** presets 7d–180d/All, default = the window the estimate was priced from, so picture and number agree. **Rendering: ImPlot 0.14 through the binding Dalamud already ships** — MIT, GPL-compatible, no new dependency; the draw list only for per-point radius, candles, glyphs, hatch and ladder. Added beyond the ticket: the Player's own recorded acts on the same axis (▲ List ▼ Reprice ★ Sold ‖ Hold, Hold runs collapsed into labelled spans — the prototype's stand-in, a Strategy replayed over History, was **dropped as not useful to the end user**), Volume and Spread sub-panes, and time-to-clear beside the ladder. **Two findings for later tickets:** the trailing-median estimator visibly lags a regime change by about half its window (a concrete target for the AR/MA clause), and `lastReviewTime` back-projection is a lower bound, so Days-of-Supply history needs EMM's own stored Snapshots (to the ingestion ticket). **New requirement on approval: the graph must pull up in an item overlay when called upon** (to the UI ticket). Ruled on review: candles stay optional; the band stays centred while the estimate trails; **"Estimate" enters the glossary** (45 terms on recount); the Days-of-Supply pane ships off by default; the layer controls are to be improved beyond the prototype for better understanding.
