82 characters. Well inside what the installer carries in practice: Allagan

## Description

> Eorzean Market Master is a marketboard companion for players who run retainers
> in earnest.
>
> Configure a pricing strategy once — undercut behaviour, a price floor, a
> minimum margin over what the item cost you — assign it to a ware or a group of
> wares, and EMM applies it across every retainer on every character you play. It
> lists, reprices and relists on its own, so upkeep is limited to enrolling new
> wares rather than revisiting old ones.
>
> Prices are drawn from the live marketboard and from Universalis, and every
> figure carries its age and the number of sales behind it. A price resting on
> three sales from a fortnight ago is labelled as such rather than quoted as a
> price. EMM also records what you paid, so it can report profit and not merely
> revenue.
>
> EMM does not start from nothing. It reads the sale and price records the market
> plugins already on your machine have accumulated and folds them into its own
> history, so there is a body of past pricing behaviour to judge a strategy
> against from the first day rather than after months of collecting.
>
> Buying is covered as well as selling: what to acquire, at what ceiling, within
> the markets you can already reach.

The AutoRetainer paragraph that stood here was cut on the maintainer's
instruction: it was honest about the dependency, but it put another plugin's
name in EMM's own store copy.

**Provisional.** The description states the destination and the decided

## Historical import — a requirement, not a candidate

Stated by the maintainer on 2026-08-17 while resolving this ticket: EMM ingests
the historical data other market plugins hold, so that it ships with enough past
pricing behaviour to serve as a test bed for strategies. This is to be **baked
into the plugin**, not left as an optional integration.

This raises what the companion-plugin research
([#4](https://github.com/local-variable/EMM/issues/4)) had recommended: a
one-time `SoldItems.csv` import was scoped as **build**, but Allagan Tools'
`market_cache.csv` was scoped as **defer**. The requirement pulls the deferred
half forward. Carried to [#9](https://github.com/local-variable/EMM/issues/9)
and noted on the map; the honesty constraint from that research still binds —
those stores are overwritten snapshots rather than append-only series, and an
imported row carries detection time rather than sale time and no cost basis, so
imported history must be labelled by source and not silently blended with
EMM's own observations.

```
marketboard, market board, market, retainer, retainers, pricing, price,

```

Dalamud's dev-plugin validation raises `NoTags` when this is empty, so it must

## Category tags

```
inventory, utility
```

Dalamud accepts exactly eight category tags — `other`, `jobs`, `ui`,
`minigames`, `inventory`, `sound`, `social`, `utility` — matched
case-insensitively against a whitelist, with anything unrecognised silently
dropped (`PluginCategoryManager.cs`). Most market plugins set none at all; of
the market-adjacent plugins installed locally only Price Insight sets any
(`ui`, `utility`), so filling these in is close to free discoverability.

`ui` is deliberately not claimed: EMM has a UI, but it is not a plugin *about*
the game's interface.

## Icon

`images/icon.png` — 512 × 512 RGBA PNG, ~47 KB. Generated by
[`assets/icon/generate_icon.py`](../assets/icon/generate_icon.py); re-run that
script rather than editing the PNG, and it re-verifies the geometry as it goes.

A summoning bell in gold on deep navy, carrying a green price series across its
face. The bell is the surface EMM lives at, which no other market plugin's icon
claims — they are coins and magnifying glasses. Original artwork: every shape
is drawn from coordinates in the generator, with nothing traced from or derived
from Square Enix assets or another plugin's icon.

### Constraints, read from the running Dalamud (`15.0.3.2`, commit `83042016d0`)

- Icons are capped at **512 × 512** and **must be square**
  (`PluginImageCache.PluginIconWidth`/`PluginIconHeight`, `requireSquare: true`).
  A violation is written to the log as an error and the icon is then simply not
  displayed — there is no user-visible failure, so confirm it renders.
- No byte-size cap exists anywhere in Dalamud, DalamudPackager, or D17.
- Store images are capped at **730 × 380**, need not be square, and are read as
  `images/image1.png` upward; more than five logs "has too many images".

### The three resolution paths

`IconUrl` is not universally consulted, so the file must both ship and be hosted:

| Install type | Where the icon comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Dev-loaded | `<directory of the DLL>/images/icon.png`, from disk. `IconUrl` is ignored. |
| Third-party repo | `IconUrl`, fetched over HTTP. |
| Mainline (D17) | A composed `PluginDistD17` raw URL. `IconUrl` is ignored. |

So the scaffold must copy `images/icon.png` next to the built DLL, or the icon
will not appear during in-game development.

`IconUrl` for the third-party repo JSON, once this file is committed and pushed:

```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/local-variable/EMM/main/images/icon.png
```
